01 / How we work

A stage-gated path from spark to scale.

Each venture earns its way forward by clearing milestones across science, demand, product, team, finance, and operations — built on a unique partnership with the UC Davis Innovation Institute for Food and Health.

01

Explore

Identify market gaps and emerging science. Validate the opportunity through market research, persona work, and expert review — then a pitch to partners.

02

Prototype

Initial brand design, in-hand tested prototype, packaging mock-ups. Secure IP and identify the founding EIR or CEO.

03

Validate

Full pre-pilot review: IP defensibility, product-market fit, brand, and consumer feedback. NewCo formation and pre-seed financing.

04

Pilot

Test the go-to-market strategy. Stand up supply chain. Evaluate team performance. Pre-seed financing from advisors and strategic partners.

05

Launch

Full product launch. First institutional financing, talent acquisition, and the operational support to scale into a category leader.

02 / Our Ventures

Ventures in motion.

Each venture began as a question at the intersection of food, biology, and consumer health — and matured through our stage-gated process into a standalone company.

Seed · 2023

KetoTap Core

Continuous ketone monitoring

The enzyme that makes continuous ketone monitoring viable. A novel BHB oxidase developed with UC Davis, Codexis, and EvoEnzyme — drop-in technology for blood meter manufacturers and continuous monitor developers, with the same electrochemistry as glucose oxidase and none of the dehydrogenase complexity.

Visit phenotap.com
Incubation · 2025

Caffree

Precision decaffeination

An enzyme-based decaffeination technology developed in partnership with UC Davis researchers. Caffree gives consumers and producers a way to dial in caffeine levels precisely without sacrificing the flavor, ritual, or health benefits that make coffee what it is to the people who drink it.

Visit caffree.com
Incubation · 2026

AminoSense

Measuring amino acid levels

A new measurement platform for amino acids — the body's most fundamental signal of recovery, growth, and aging. Currently in stealth, with a science team incubated through Barnstorm and a technology stack rooted in UC Davis biochemistry research.
