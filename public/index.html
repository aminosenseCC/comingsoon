Workflow

Sample to insight in
three simple steps

1

Prepare & Load

Add 10 μL of your sample — serum, cell lysate, media, or buffer — directly onto the AminoSense cartridge. No derivatization or pre-treatment required.

2

Measure

Insert the cartridge into the reader. The biosensor delivers a readout in as little as 30 seconds.

3

Analyze & Export

View concentration instantly on the integrated display. Export data to our research portal using USB-C or Bluetooth.
